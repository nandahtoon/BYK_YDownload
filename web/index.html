How to Download a Complete Coursera Course:

  1. Enroll in the course on coursera.org in your browser.
  2. Export your cookies: Install a browser extension like Cookie-Editor or Get-cookies.txt, export your cookies for coursera.org in Netscape (.txt) format, and save the file.
  3. Select your cookies file above using the "Select Cookies File (.txt)" button.
  4. Paste the Course URL or Slug (e.g. https://www.coursera.org/learn/machine-learning or just machine-learning) and click "Download Complete Course".
